Built for speed and privacy

No accounts, no tracking — your data never leaves the tab.

100% client-side

Every tool runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded (except optional QR generation).

Command palette

Press ⌘/Ctrl+K to jump to any tool or action; / to quick-open, ⌘↵ to run.

Light & dark

A refined dev-tool theme with light, dark and system modes — easy on the eyes, day or night.

Five languages

Fully translated interface in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German.

Open source

Astro + Solid.js + TypeScript (strict) + Tailwind v4. MIT licensed. Contributions welcome.

Favorites & recents

Star the tools you use most and jump back into recent ones — all stored locally.
